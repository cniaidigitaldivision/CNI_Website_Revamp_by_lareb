About Crescent Nova International

A legacy built on trust, and an ambition that keeps the Kingdom's future unforgettable

Crescent Nova International is the Saudi-registered subsidiary of the Attari Group — a Pakistani conglomerate with 18 years of dispute-free business excellence, now building a multi-industry investment platform at the heart of Vision 2030.

Our Vision

To become a leading multi-industry investment platform in KSA, enabling global partnerships and sustainable growth across sectors that drive Saudi Arabia's economic transformation under Vision 2030.

18 Years of Proven Business Excellence

From a regional enterprise in Wah Cantt to a diversified conglomerate — every phase grounded in proven revenue, not speculation.

2007

Foundation in Wah Cantt

Initial formation launching regional engineering, supply chain, and trading contracts.

2013

Al Maida Frozen Foods

Establishment of Al Maida Frozen Foods, building nationwide industrial cold chain logistics.

2019

Executive Housing Project

Delivery of premier residential communities, mastering land title verification and development execution.

2022

Town Square D-17

Capital project delivery in Islamabad D-17 sector — mixed-use commercial facilities.

2023–24

Investo21 & Daniyal Marketing

Digital transformation through Investo21 technology platforms and nationwide Daniyal Marketing operations.

2025–26

Chitral Royal Homes & CNI KSA

Official incorporation of Crescent Nova International in Riyadh and launch of Chitral Royal luxury resort homes.
