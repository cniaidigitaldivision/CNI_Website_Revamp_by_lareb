INVESTOR FAQS

Common Questions.
Clear Answers.

Are projected returns guaranteed? +
No, projected returns are targets based on historical data and strategic modelling. Investment capital is at risk, and actual returns may vary.
What are the available investment tiers? +
We offer Silver (SAR 500k+), Gold (SAR 2M+), and Platinum (SAR 10M+) tiers, each with distinct horizons and target ROIs.
What is the minimum investment? +
The minimum capital requirement starts at SAR 500,000 for the Silver tier.
What risks should investors consider? +
Investments carry market, liquidity, and operational risks. Full risk disclosures are provided in the official investment documentation upon engagement.
What documents are required? +
Standard KYC, proof of funds, and signed investment agreements are required. The exact documentation varies by investor profile and jurisdiction.
How can investors review exit terms? +
Detailed withdrawal, liquidity events, and exit clauses are clearly defined in the final investment agreement provided during the review phase.
