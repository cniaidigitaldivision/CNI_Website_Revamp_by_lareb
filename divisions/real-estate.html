Division Overview

Where Local Expertise Meets Investment Intelligence

CNI's Real Estate & Advisory division is built on the deep property expertise of the Attari Group, combining local market knowledge with digital-first property marketing to serve KSA's growing real estate demand. With 2.5M+ Pakistani residents in the Kingdom seeking residential and commercial properties, the opportunity to facilitate and advise on transactions is substantial and growing.

Our services span the full property lifecycle — from digital property listings and marketing to Absher ID facilitation for transactions, residential and commercial renovation, and strategic advisory for Pakistani and international investors looking to enter the KSA property market.

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 agenda is directly fuelling a construction and housing boom across Riyadh, Jeddah, and the Eastern Province. CNI's Real Estate division is positioned at the centre of this growth — offering a trusted, knowledgeable, and fully regulated pathway into the Kingdom's property market.

Our Services

01

Property Marketing & Digital Listings

Professional listings and targeted digital marketing for residential and commercial properties across Riyadh, Jeddah, and the Eastern Province. High-quality photography, virtual tours, and multi-platform distribution.

02

Transaction & Absher Facilitation

Complete support for property transactions including Absher ID verification, Ministry of Justice coordination, and all regulatory filings required for expatriate and Saudi national property transfers.

03

Residential & Commercial Renovation

End-to-end renovation management — from interior design and contracting to project oversight and final delivery. We transform properties to maximise rental yield and resale value.

04

Investment Advisory

Strategic guidance for Pakistani and international investors navigating KSA's property regulations, ownership structures, and high-yield opportunities — from off-plan to commercial assets.
