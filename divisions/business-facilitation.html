Division Overview

End-to-End Market Entry

CNI's Business Facilitation Centre is an end-to-end platform built to help Pakistani and international investors enter, establish, and scale their operations in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with confidence and full legal compliance. From initial feasibility assessment to company registration, Saudi licensing, and ongoing PRO services, we manage the entire setup journey so investors can focus on their business rather than bureaucracy.

Our joint-venture structuring service is particularly powerful for investors seeking a trusted local partner. CNI acts as the managing partner in JV arrangements, offering equity participation ranging from 30% to 51%, backed by quarterly reporting, ROI monitoring, and full compliance support. This model has been designed to align CNI's incentives directly with investor success.

Whether you are registering a new entity, restructuring an existing operation, or exploring a joint-venture, CNI's Business Facilitation Centre provides the local expertise, regulatory relationships, and operational infrastructure to make your market entry seamless, compliant, and profitable.

The CNI JV Model

Partnership That Works For You

In CNI's JV model, you bring the capital and we bring everything else: local licensing, operational management, government relationships, and market expertise. The equity split ranges from 30% to 51% in your favor, meaning you retain majority ownership while CNI handles the complexity of day-to-day operations in the Kingdom.

Every JV includes quarterly reporting with full financial transparency, dedicated ROI monitoring, and ongoing compliance management. CNI's incentives are directly aligned with your success — we grow when you grow. This model is ideal for investors who want passive, compliant returns from the Saudi market without relocating or building a local team from scratch.
