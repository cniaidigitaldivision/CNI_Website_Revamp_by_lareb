Division Overview

The Intelligence Engine

CNI's AI & Digital Division is the intelligence engine behind the entire CNI ecosystem — building and deploying AI solutions, enterprise software, and digital growth strategies first for CNI's own divisions, then scaling those proven capabilities to external clients across Saudi Arabia. This inside-out model means every solution we offer has been battle-tested in a real business environment before it reaches a client.

From intelligent automation and CRM systems to WhatsApp sales bots, digital marketing, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity — the AI & Digital Division delivers end-to-end digital transformation that produces measurable business outcomes. We don't just sell software; we deliver performance.
