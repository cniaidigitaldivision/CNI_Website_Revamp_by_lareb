Division Overview

Premium Accommodation Solutions

CNI's Hospitality Management division delivers premium accommodation and hospitality solutions for corporate travelers, business delegations, and the Pakistani diaspora performing Hajj and Umrah. Leveraging CNI's established relationships with Saudi hospitality providers, the division specializes in securing hotel management contracts, negotiating bulk corporate accommodation deals, and creating specialized packages for Muslim pilgrims.

The Pakistani diaspora and Muslim world represent one of the largest and most loyal hospitality markets in KSA — with millions traveling each year for religious obligations, business, and family visits. CNI's deep community ties and on-ground presence in the Kingdom position us uniquely to capture this demand, offering unmatched reliability and culturally attuned service.

Service Pillars

Corporate Accommodation Solutions

Long-term and short-term accommodation contracts tailored for businesses, trade delegations, and relocated employees across major Saudi cities.

Hotel Management Contracts

Operational management partnerships with hotel properties across KSA, ensuring high occupancy rates, operational excellence, and premium guest experiences.

Curated, end-to-end packages for pilgrims encompassing flights, luxury accommodation near the Haram, ground transport, and dedicated guide services.
