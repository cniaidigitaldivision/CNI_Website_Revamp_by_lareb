Division Overview

Travel Rooted in Trust & Community

CNI's Tour & Travel division is dedicated to serving the Pakistani diaspora and Muslim community in KSA with comprehensive, affordable, and spiritually enriching travel services. From Umrah packages to airline ticketing, hotel bookings, and group tours — we are a one-stop platform built on trust, relationships, and deep knowledge of KSA's travel ecosystem.

Our business model is built on commission-based revenue, earning 8–15% per transaction across all service lines — a capital-light model with high transaction volume potential given the 2.5M+ strong Pakistani community in the Kingdom. As Umrah visitor numbers continue to grow toward Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 target of 30 million pilgrims annually, the opportunity for our division scales proportionally.

Every journey we facilitate is handled with the same care and attention that our clients deserve — from the first inquiry call to the final return flight. CNI's Tour & Travel team combines local KSA expertise with an intimate understanding of what Pakistani families, first-time pilgrims, and corporate groups need from a travel partner.
