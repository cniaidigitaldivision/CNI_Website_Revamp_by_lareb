Division Overview

The GCC's Smart Logistics Hub

My Truck is CNI's shared-load freight platform, connecting shippers with verified carriers across Saudi Arabia on a transparent, technology-driven 15% platform-fee model. As the Kingdom scales its role as the GCC's dominant logistics hub under Vision 2030, the demand for efficient, cost-optimized freight solutions is accelerating rapidly — and My Truck is built to capture this momentum.

The platform's shared-load model optimizes truck utilization, reducing empty miles and passing the savings on to shippers as lower freight costs. Traditional logistics models involve significant deadhead (empty returns) and rigid dedicated-truck pricing, making freight expensive for SME shippers and inefficient for carriers.

For carriers, My Truck provides a steady pipeline of verified loads, live tracking tools, and secure payment processing — all accessible via a mobile-first app designed in Arabic, English, and Urdu to serve the diverse driver workforce in KSA. By connecting fragmented capacity with digital efficiency, My Truck moves cargo smarter.

How It Works

Three steps from pickup to delivery

1

Shipper Posts a Load

Shippers enter pickup location, destination, cargo type, and weight via the app. Instant pricing is generated based on shared-load availability.

2

Matched to Verified Carrier

The platform algorithm instantly matches the load to the nearest available, background-checked carrier moving in that direction with capacity.

3

Delivered & Tracked

Live GPS tracking provides full visibility from pickup to drop-off. Once delivery is confirmed, payment is processed automatically via the app.
