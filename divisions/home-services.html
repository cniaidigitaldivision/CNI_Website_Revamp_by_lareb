Division Overview

Home Maintenance, Reimagined

Mundus is CNI's on-demand home-services marketplace, connecting vetted, background-checked maintenance technicians with residential and commercial clients across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Built for the mobile-first Saudi consumer, Mundus addresses a genuine gap in the market — reliable, trustworthy, and fairly priced home maintenance at the tap of a button.

The platform operates on a 15–25% commission model, meaning no upfront cost for service providers and no risk for clients. Technicians are rigorously vetted and background-checked before onboarding, ensuring quality and safety standards that Saudi households and expatriate communities have come to demand.

Mundus is positioned to become the trusted home services brand in KSA — a market currently dominated by informal, fragmented, and unreliable service providers. Our commitment to quality control, transparent pricing, and mobile convenience sets Mundus apart in a region where 95%+ of consumers are smartphone-first.

How It Works

Three steps from booking to job complete

1

Client Books via App

Browse available services, select a vetted technician, pick a time slot, and confirm your booking — all in under 2 minutes on your smartphone.

2

Vetted Technician Dispatched

A background-checked, fully vetted Mundus-certified professional arrives at your location at the scheduled time, equipped and ready.

3

Job Complete, Payment Processed

Secure in-app payment is processed upon job completion. The technician receives 75–85% of the fee; Mundus earns 15–25% as platform commission.

Business Model

Commission-Based, Capital-Light

Mundus earns 15–25% of every transaction processed through the platform. There is no inventory, no equipment cost, and no overhead risk. The model scales purely with network growth — more technicians and more clients means more revenue, with zero additional fixed cost.

With an estimated average ticket size of SAR 300–800 per job and a technician network scaling across Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam, Mundus is designed to compound in value as the community grows.

Market Opportunity

A fragmented, high-demand market with no dominant trusted brand yet — the perfect conditions for Mundus

Saudi Household Demand

Saudi households have strong, year-round demand for reliable home maintenance. The hot climate alone drives constant HVAC, plumbing, and electrical call-outs. Existing providers are fragmented, informal, and unreliable — Mundus fills this gap with a professional, vetted alternative.

2.5M+ Pakistani Residents + Global Expat Community

The expatriate population in KSA exceeds 10M people — with over 2.5M Pakistanis alone. This community, unfamiliar with local contractors and wary of informal providers, actively seeks a trusted, vetted home services solution exactly like Mundus.

95%+
Smartphone Penetration in KSA

Saudi Arabia is one of the world's most mobile-first markets. Consumers are comfortable booking services, paying in-app, and leaving reviews on their smartphones — Mundus is perfectly aligned with this consumer behaviour.

Fragmented Market — No Dominant Brand

Unlike ride-hailing (Uber/Careem), KSA's home services market has no dominant trusted brand. The first mover to establish credibility, vetting standards, and mobile convenience at scale will capture significant market share — and Mundus is that first mover.
