Division Overview

Driving Excellence in the Kingdom

CNI's Automotive Division operates a TGA-licensed premium vehicle rental fleet designed to serve tourism, corporate, and expatriate markets across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Every vehicle in the fleet is GPS-tracked through the WASL platform, fully insured, and maintained to the highest safety and compliance standards. The division holds Green+ Nitaqat certification, ensuring full regulatory alignment with Saudi labor and business requirements.

The flagship division of the CNI portfolio, Automotive delivers the strongest financial performance with a projected 62.5% net profit margin on a SAR 1,000,000 Phase 1 fleet investment — generating SAR 600,000 in annual net profit. Phase 1 targets a 10-vehicle fleet, with phased expansion as B2B contracts with corporate clients and tourism operators are secured.
