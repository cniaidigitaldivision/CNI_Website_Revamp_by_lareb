Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, foreign investors can establish companies in Saudi Arabia. This is typically facilitated through a foreign investment license issued by the Ministry of Investment (MISA). Eligibility depends on the nature of the business and fulfilling specific capital and operational requirements.

A wide range of sectors is open to international investment, including technology, manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, and consulting. However, some specific sectors may have restrictions or require a minimum percentage of local ownership.

Generally, a MISA license and a Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce are required. Depending on the activity, additional sector-specific licenses (e.g., from the Communications, Space & Technology Commission for IT, or the Ministry of Tourism) may be mandatory.

Yes, 100% foreign ownership is permitted in many sectors, including services, manufacturing, and trading (subject to high capital requirements for trading). Certain restricted sectors still mandate a Saudi partner.
